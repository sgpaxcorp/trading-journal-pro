# Complete NeuroTrader User Manual

## Access
- Help Center -> Complete Manual.
- Private workspace -> Help icon or Neuro Guide -> ask for any module by name.

This page is the complete operating guide for NeuroTrader. It explains what each area is, what it is used for, how to reach it, how to use it, and what a good workflow looks like.

NeuroTrader is designed as a trading business operating system. The platform does not replace your judgment, does not execute trades, and does not provide financial advice. It helps you plan, document, measure, review, and improve your trading process with structured data and AI-assisted accountability.

## Core operating loop
The platform is built around one loop:

1. Plan the business.
2. Prepare the session.
3. Execute according to the plan.
4. Record what happened.
5. Review the evidence.
6. Adjust one thing at a time.

Every page exists to support one part of that loop. If a page feels confusing, ask: does this help me plan, execute, journal, review, or improve?

## Navigation map
Most private pages are reached from the top navigation after login.

**Business Performance** contains business results, analytics, cash flow, P&L, AI coaching, and plan summaries.

**Business Notebook** contains your playbook, rules, lessons, and written operating notes.

**Strategy Review Lab** is where you replay and inspect strategy behavior.

**Smart Tools** contains private beta tools such as Option Flows Analysis and Neuro Analysis.

**Business Protection System** contains rules, alarms, reminders, and routine checks.

**Account menu** contains profile, preferences, security, billing, and billing history.

## Dashboard / Business Center
**What it is:** The daily command center for your trading business.

**What it is used for:** It gives you a quick view of plan progress, recent activity, risk status, discipline, and important reminders.

**How to get there:** Login -> Dashboard.

**How to use it:** Start your day here. Check whether your current account progress, session data, and business reminders are aligned with your plan. If numbers look empty, create or import journal data first.

**What good use looks like:** You can answer three questions before trading: What is my target today? What is my risk limit? What behavior must I avoid?

## Trading Business Plan / Growth Plan
**What it is:** The source of truth for your business objective, capital path, timeline, trading days, risk limits, and checkpoint pacing.

**What it is used for:** It translates a long-term goal into measurable daily, weekly, and monthly checkpoints.

**How to get there:** Business Performance -> Trading Business Plan or Growth Plan.

**How to use it:** Enter starting balance, target balance, target date, trading days, max daily loss, and risk assumptions. Save the plan before relying on dashboards or projections.

**What good use looks like:** Your plan is realistic enough to follow and strict enough to protect capital. If your daily target requires risk you cannot safely take, revise the plan instead of forcing trades.

## Journal / Execution Records
**What it is:** The record of your trading sessions, decisions, trades, emotions, and post-session truth.

**What it is used for:** It converts behavior into data so analytics and AI coaching have evidence to work from.

**How to get there:** Journal or Daily Session from the private workspace.

**How to use it:** Complete premarket, execution, and review sections. Save the session. Attach screenshots or notes when they explain a decision.

**What good use looks like:** A stranger could read your journal and understand your plan, what you did, why you did it, and what you learned.

## Neuro Layer
**What it is:** The psychology and decision-quality layer inside the Journal.

**What it is used for:** It tracks triggers, emotional state, discipline, plan deviation, hesitation, revenge trading risk, and post-trade self-awareness.

**How to get there:** Journal -> Neuro Layer sections.

**How to use it:** Fill it before, during, and after trading. Do not treat it like a diary only; treat it like structured behavior data.

**What good use looks like:** You can identify the conditions that lead to good execution and the conditions that lead to rule-breaking.

## Analytics & Business Performance
**What it is:** The measurement area for P&L, consistency, win rate, expectancy, drawdown, strategy behavior, and performance patterns.

**What it is used for:** It shows whether your actual behavior is producing the business result you expected.

**How to get there:** Business Performance -> Business Analytics or balance/performance pages.

**How to use it:** Select the correct date range. Confirm that sessions and imports exist. Review one pattern at a time instead of trying to fix everything.

**What good use looks like:** You leave with one decision: repeat, reduce, stop, or investigate a behavior.

## KPIs
**What they are:** Business measurements that summarize performance and risk.

**What they are used for:** They help you separate outcome quality from decision quality.

**How to get there:** Analytics pages and Help Center -> KPIs.

**How to use them:** Use KPIs as evidence, not ego. A high win rate can still lose money if losers are too large. A lower win rate can be profitable if expectancy is strong.

**What good use looks like:** You understand which metric changed, why it changed, and what action it requires.

## Business AI Coach
**What it is:** An AI review layer that uses your saved data to produce coaching, patterns, and action items.

**What it is used for:** It helps you convert journal evidence, analytics, and plan context into a specific improvement plan.

**How to get there:** Business Performance -> Business AI Coach.

**How to use it:** Run coaching after you have enough saved sessions. Ask for patterns, rules to reinforce, rules to remove, and one action for the next week.

**What good use looks like:** You receive a short action plan grounded in your own data, then you test that plan in future sessions.

## Business Notebook
**What it is:** Your written playbook.

**What it is used for:** It stores rules, setups, lessons, checklists, strategy notes, review notes, and repeatable processes.

**How to get there:** Business Notebook in the top navigation.

**How to use it:** Create pages for setups, risk rules, screenshots, weekly lessons, and repeatable checklists. Keep it organized by topic.

**What good use looks like:** Your notebook becomes the clean version of what your journal taught you.

## Strategy Review Lab / Back-Study
**What it is:** A visual review area for setups, charts, entry/exit decisions, and repeatable strategy behavior.

**What it is used for:** It helps validate whether a setup was real, whether execution matched the plan, and whether the strategy deserves more capital or less attention.

**How to get there:** Strategy Review Lab in the top navigation.

**How to use it:** Review charts and decisions after the session. Compare planned entry, actual entry, stop, target, and management decisions.

**What good use looks like:** You identify one execution improvement and one strategy rule to keep, change, or remove.

## Business Protection System
**What it is:** The rules, alarms, reminders, and routine checks that protect you from avoidable mistakes.

**What it is used for:** It turns risk rules into visible operating guardrails.

**How to get there:** Business Protection System -> Alarms or Reminders.

**How to use it:** Create reminders for premarket routine, max loss, review time, journaling, and weekly audits. Use alarms for behaviors that can damage the account.

**What good use looks like:** Your rules appear before the mistake, not after it.

## Data Inputs & Imports
**What it is:** The data ingestion area for broker files, CSV uploads, and supported broker sync features.

**What it is used for:** It gives analytics and reports accurate trade data.

**How to get there:** Data imports, broker import, or related import links.

**How to use it:** Import official broker history when possible. Do not edit CSV exports unless the import template requires it. Verify row counts and date range.

**What good use looks like:** The imported trades match your broker history and appear in analytics for the correct date range.

## Business P&L Office
**What it is:** The non-trade business finance area.

**What it is used for:** It tracks platform expenses, subscriptions, vendors, education costs, renewals, and break-even math.

**How to get there:** Business Performance -> Business P&L Office.

**How to use it:** Add recurring and one-time expenses. Review monthly cost, annual cost, renewal dates, and whether the business can justify each tool.

**What good use looks like:** You know the true cost of operating the trading business, not just trading P&L.

## Reports
**What it is:** Exportable summaries and review packets.

**What it is used for:** It turns platform data into structured documents for review, records, coaching, or business accountability.

**How to get there:** Reports areas, Option Flow reports, or module-specific export buttons.

**How to use it:** Generate reports after data is clean. Use them for weekly review and decision meetings with yourself.

**What good use looks like:** The report tells a clear story: objective, evidence, result, risk, and next action.

## Option Flows Analysis
**What it is:** A private beta Smart Tool for converting options flow files or screenshots into a structured premarket report.

**What it is used for:** It helps organize flow context, key contracts, aggressive activity, scenario levels, and post-session review.

**How to get there:** Smart Tools -> Option Flow or footer -> Option Flow login.

**How to use it:** Upload supported files or screenshots, generate the report, review the summary, validate with your own chart, and send useful context to the Journal.

**What good use looks like:** The report becomes a filter and preparation document, not a trade signal.

## Neuro Analysis
**What it is:** A private investment research portal for long-term company and ETF analysis.

**What it is used for:** It helps create company profiles, analyze filings, track thesis changes, evaluate portfolio holdings, model dividends and compounding, and ask grounded research questions.

**How to get there:** Smart Tools -> Neuro Analysis or footer -> Neuro Analysis login.

**How to use it:** Create a company or ETF profile by ticker. Add filings such as 10-K and 10-Q documents. For a company, complete three separate pre-valuation dossiers: the price-blind 20-dimension Business Quality Analysis, the documented Management and Capital Allocation Analysis, and the multi-year Earnings Quality and Accounting Risk Analysis. Then review direct valuation, Reverse DCF market-implied expectations, the independent adversarial Bear Case, thesis notes, portfolio impact, the evidence-linked Portfolio Exposure Map, the Macro Context Agent, the Capital Allocation Dashboard, and the Investment Committee Packet. The exposure map identifies shared economic drivers across holdings without treating ticker count or sector labels as diversification. The Macro Context Agent keeps observed data, market expectations, third-party forecasts, and AI interpretation separate while documenting how material variables reach each company's economics; it cannot issue market-timing or BUY/SELL instructions from a forecast. The allocation dashboard compares the same dollar across positions, candidates, cash, and policy-supported short-term Treasury alternatives without ranking them or requiring cash deployment. If an APPROVED idea is actually purchased outside the platform, record the real date, price, and portfolio weight to freeze the permanent Original Investment Thesis. Later analyses append source-linked thesis classifications and a Position Exit Review that compares the original thesis, valuation, and financial expectations with current evidence. Neither record rewrites the original or makes an automatic trading decision; price movement alone cannot establish thesis success or failure.

**What good use looks like:** You can explain the evidence for and against every business-quality conclusion, each material management action and financial consequence, every accounting divergence with its raw values and formula, and multiple combinations that could justify today's market value. Reverse DCF assumptions are compared with documented history and dated industry evidence without turning demanding or conservative assumptions into an automatic trade conclusion.

## Billing, Terms, and Privacy Agreements
**What it is:** The subscription and account access area.

**What it is used for:** It manages plan selection, billing cycle, trial disclosure, automatic renewal, payment status, invoices, cancellation, terms acceptance, and privacy agreement.

**How to get there:** Account menu -> Billing, public Pricing page, signup flow, or footer login links.

**How to use it:** Before first checkout, read and accept the Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, and subscription disclosures. The checkout will not continue unless all required agreements are accepted.

**What good use looks like:** You understand the trial, renewal, no-refund policy, educational-use disclosure, and how to cancel before subscribing.

## Account, Preferences, and Security
**What it is:** Your profile and app settings area.

**What it is used for:** It controls identity, language, theme, password, billing links, and account-level preferences.

**How to get there:** Avatar/account menu in the top navigation.

**How to use it:** Keep email and profile information accurate. Use Preferences for language and theme. Use Security & Privacy for password-related actions.

**What good use looks like:** Your account is easy to identify, secure, and configured in the language/theme you prefer.

## Daily workflow
Use this simple daily rhythm:

1. Open Dashboard.
2. Review Growth Plan and max loss.
3. Write premarket plan in Journal.
4. Check rules and reminders.
5. Trade only if conditions match.
6. Complete execution notes.
7. Review Neuro Layer.
8. Save the session.
9. Check Analytics after enough data exists.

## Weekly workflow
Use this weekly rhythm:

1. Review Analytics and KPIs.
2. Review Business P&L and upcoming renewals.
3. Run AI Coaching.
4. Update Notebook with lessons.
5. Adjust one rule or one process.
6. Do not change everything at once.

## If something looks wrong
Check these in order:

1. Are you logged into the right account?
2. Is the selected date range correct?
3. Did you save the session?
4. Did the import finish?
5. Is the feature private beta or disabled?
6. Does your subscription include the feature?
7. Did you accept required agreements for subscription checkout?

## Important boundaries
NeuroTrader is educational and operational software. It helps you organize data, review behavior, and structure decisions. It does not guarantee profits, does not provide legal, tax, financial, or investment advice, and does not place trades for you.
