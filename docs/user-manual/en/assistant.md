# Neuro Guide

## What Neuro Guide is
Neuro Guide is the help assistant available throughout your private workspace. Open it with the floating brain button at the bottom of the screen.

It is designed to answer three types of questions:

1. **Manual questions:** how a feature works or where to find it.
2. **Current-page questions:** what the page you are viewing is for and what to do next.
3. **Performance questions:** a concise explanation of your latest available journal, analytics, and Trading Business Plan information.

## How to ask a useful question
Use natural language. You do not need to know the exact name of a metric or page.

Examples:

- “What should I complete on this page?”
- “Explain profit factor in simple terms.”
- “Why is my dashboard missing information?”
- “How am I doing compared with my plan?”
- “Where do I record my premarket preparation?”

For better performance answers, keep your active trading account selected and make sure your Journal and imported trades are up to date.

## What performance information it can use
When your question is about your results, Neuro Guide may use a compact summary of your own active account, including:

- latest available performance period
- saved sessions and trades
- total P&L and average result
- win rate, profit factor, and expectancy when available
- recent journal results and plan-respect answers
- starting capital, target, and latest Trading Business Plan settings

It only uses information associated with your signed-in account. If the required information is missing or old, it tells you instead of guessing.

## Neuro Guide and Business AI Coach
Use **Neuro Guide** for quick orientation, feature explanations, and a concise reading of your latest results.

Use **Business AI Coach** for a deeper coaching conversation, pattern analysis, execution review, and an action plan based on more complete evidence.

## Conversation controls
- Use the quick questions to start without typing.
- Continue asking follow-up questions in the same conversation.
- Select **Clear** to start over.
- Select a manual source below an answer to open the related Help Center page.

The conversation in the panel is temporary and is cleared when you refresh or select Clear.

## Important limits
Neuro Guide is an educational and business-performance assistant. It does not promise results, place trades, tell you what security to buy or sell, or replace professional financial, legal, accounting, or tax advice.

Your projected Trading Business Plan is a planning model. Your actual result depends on execution, losses, costs, withdrawals, contributions, and market conditions.

## If the answer is not enough
1. Open the suggested manual source.
2. Confirm that the correct trading account is active.
3. Update your Journal or import if performance data is missing.
4. Use Business AI Coach when the question requires deeper analysis.
5. Contact Support when the issue concerns billing, access, an incorrect charge, or a technical problem that the manual cannot resolve.
